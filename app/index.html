Library

    An offline library for bilingual Japanese–English reader files. Books stay on this device; each one remembers its own reading position and display settings.

    Tap Import and pick a reader .html file. Importing a book you already have replaces it and keeps your place.

    Sample: Akutagawa Ryūnosuke, 蜘蛛の糸 (1918), public domain, Japanese text from Aozora Bunko.
